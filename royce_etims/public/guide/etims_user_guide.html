eTIMS User Guide

How to set up KRA eTIMS, register your items, and sell with KRA-signed invoices and till receipts. Every sale you submit is sent to KRA in the background, and KRA's signature, CU invoice number and QR code are printed on the invoice or receipt.

The screenshots come from a test system connected to KRA's sandbox, so they show a test KRA PIN and test product categories. Your own system shows your PIN and KRA's full lists.

1. Before you start

eTIMS is set up once per company, usually by your ERP support team together with your accountant. You need:

  • Your company's KRA PIN.
  • Your KRA branch ID. Your head office is branch 00. Other shops registered with KRA have their own IDs (01, 02, ...). Use only branches KRA has registered for your PIN.
  • Your GavaConnect app keys (Consumer Key, Consumer Secret and Apigee App ID). KRA issues these once your integration is approved. Type them into eTIMS Settings yourself, and don't send them by email or chat.
  • A role that can change settings (System Manager or Accounts Manager) for sections 2 to 5. Cashiers only need sections 8 and 10.

Everything is under KRA eTIMS in the left-hand sidebar. The sidebar opens on the eTIMS Dashboard.

2. Add your KRA branch

Open KRA eTIMS > Branches and click + Add eTIMS Branch.

  1. Company: your company.
  2. Branch: the ERPNext branch (the shop or office).
  3. Branch ID (bhfId): the ID KRA gave this branch, for example 00 for head office.
  4. Leave Device Serial No empty. It's filled in for you.
  5. Save, then click Register Device and confirm. KRA registers the device and returns its keys. The branch then shows Device Status: Registered (or Active) and its CU ID.
Register a device once. If registration fails, don't keep clicking: note the message and contact your support team.

4. Check the connection and activate

  1. Click Test Connection. KRA should recognise your branch and show your business name.
  2. Click Sync KRA Reference Data. This downloads KRA's lists: VAT bands, units, packaging, countries, product categories and credit-note reasons. It also runs every week by itself.
  3. Set Default KRA Product Category (type a word to search KRA's list) and Save.
  4. Click Activate eTIMS and confirm. From now on every sale is sent to KRA.
Activating switches the whole system to Commercial Rounding (half a cent rounds up), the way KRA rounds. Without it, an invoice can differ from KRA by a cent and KRA refuses it.

Disable eTIMS stops sending sales to KRA. Use it only on your support team's or KRA's advice.

5. Register your items

KRA signs a sale only if every item on it is registered with KRA under a KRA item code (for example KE2NTNO0000088).

All items at once

On eTIMS Settings, click Register All Items with KRA and confirm. It runs in the background: about 1,000 items take 15 to 20 minutes, and you can keep working. For each item it:

  • fills empty KRA fields: product category, item type, VAT band, country of origin, packaging and quantity unit. It uses what ERPNext already knows, then the Item Defaults from section 3, and never changes a value already on the item;
  • registers the item with KRA;
  • tells KRA how much stock you hold of it, so it can be sold straight away.

When it finishes, a summary appears in the Activity at the bottom of eTIMS Settings, listing any items KRA didn't accept and why.

One item

Open the item, check its KRA eTIMS tab, and click Sync to eTIMS. Do this for every new item before you sell it.

Units KRA doesn't accept

KRA's item codes need a two-letter quantity unit. Pieces (NO), packets (PA), rolls (RO), bags (BG), boxes (BX), pairs (PR), dozens (DZ) and kilograms (KG) work. Metres, litres and feet have no two-letter KRA code, so items sold in those units are refused with "KRA only accepts item codes with a 2-letter quantity unit". Agree with your accountant which unit to use (for example NO or RO), set it in the item's KRA eTIMS tab, and click Sync to eTIMS again.

6. Stock and KRA

With Sync Stock Movements ticked, stock coming in or going out is sent to KRA by itself: purchase receipts, stock entries, stock reconciliations and the stock part of sales. You don't need to do anything. Each one appears under KRA eTIMS > Stock Movements with its status.

If one shows Failed, open it to read KRA's reason. Movements go to KRA in order, so one failure holds back the ones after it until it's fixed. Ask your support team if the reason isn't clear.

7. Sell with a Sales Invoice

  1. Create the Sales Invoice as usual: customer, items, quantities. If the customer has a KRA PIN, set it as the customer's Tax ID, so KRA records the sale against their PIN.
  2. Check the VAT under Taxes and Charges. There should be one Output VAT row.
  3. Save and Submit.
  4. Within a few seconds the eTIMS Status changes from Pending to Sent, and the eTIMS Receipt section shows KRA's signature. The View at KRA button opens KRA's own record of the sale.
  5. Print or email it. The Kenya Tax Invoice print shows KRA's CU invoice number, CU ID, signature and QR code.
A submitted invoice can't be edited or cancelled once KRA has signed it. To correct a sale, issue a credit note (section 9).

8. Sell at the till (POS)

Once per till

Open the till's POS Profile and set Print Format to Kenya POS Receipt. That receipt carries KRA's details and QR code. A receipt layout of your own won't show them.

Every sale

  1. Open the till (Point of Sale) as usual and add the items.
  2. Click Checkout, choose the payment method (cash or M-Pesa), and Complete Order.
  3. The sale goes to KRA straight away. Click Print Receipt: the till waits a few seconds for KRA's signature, then prints the receipt with the QR code.
If the receipt says "KRA signature pending", KRA hadn't answered yet. The sale is still sent. Reprint it a minute later from Recent Orders so the customer gets the QR code.

9. Credit notes (returns)

  1. Open the signed Sales Invoice and click Create > Return / Credit Note.
  2. Adjust the quantities returned (they show as negative numbers).
  3. Choose the eTIMS Refund Reason, for example 06 Refund, 03 Damaged or 09 Wrong Amount/price. KRA requires one.
  4. Save and Submit. ERPNext may show a note about "Update Outstanding for Self"; close it. The credit note goes to KRA and is signed like an invoice.

10. When KRA hasn't signed a sale

A sale that KRA hasn't signed shows eTIMS Status: Failed with KRA's reason underneath. The system retries every 15 minutes by itself. Once you've fixed the cause, click Retry eTIMS on the invoice to send it straight away.

KRA's messageWhat to do

Item ... does not exist in your stock masterKRA has no stock for the item. Open the item and click Sync to eTIMS (it sends the stock you hold), or check Stock Movements for a failed one. Then Retry eTIMS.

11. Purchases from eTIMS suppliers

When a supplier who uses eTIMS sells to you, KRA records the sale against your PIN. Every hour these are fetched into KRA eTIMS > Purchases. Open one, check it, and click Create Purchase Invoice. When you submit the Purchase Invoice, the purchase is confirmed with KRA. Use Reject with KRA for a purchase that isn't yours or is wrong. Goods you import are listed under Imported Items to approve or reject in the same way.

13. Daily checklist

  • Open the eTIMS Dashboard. Waiting for KRA and Stock Not Sent to KRA should be 0 by the end of the day. If not, open Compliance Status and fix each line (section 10).
  • Register every new item before it's sold: Sync to eTIMS on the item.
  • Check that customers get receipts with the KRA QR code. Reprint any "KRA signature pending" receipts.
  • At month end, compare the VAT Summary with your VAT return.
